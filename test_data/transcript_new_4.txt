Interview Title:    Customer Discovery Call — Banking Platform Migration
Interview ID:       CD-2024-0233
Date:              July 9, 2024
Time:              11:00 – 12:15 IST
Location:          Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
Interviewer:       Anjali Deshmukh (Product Manager, FinServe Technologies)
Interviewee:       Rohan Mehta (Chief Technology Officer, Bharat Cooperative Bank)
Observer:          Kenji Watanabe (Solutions Engineer, FinServe Technologies)
=====================================================================

[11:00:10] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Good morning, Rohan. Thank you for taking the time. Kenji from our
engineering team is joining us. We'd like to understand your
current infrastructure before we propose a migration plan.

[11:00:35] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Happy to help, Anjali. Just to set context — Bharat Cooperative
Bank has 247 branches across 12 states. We serve approximately
4.2 million retail customers and 38,000 corporate clients. Our
core banking system is Finacle, version 10.2.18, running on
IBM AIX 7.2.

[11:01:20] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
That's an older Finacle version. When was the last major upgrade?

[11:01:32] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
2019. We attempted an upgrade to Finacle 11 in March 2022, but
it was rolled back after four days due to data reconciliation
failures. The vendor — Infosys — sent a team of 14 engineers
from their Pune and Bangalore offices. The project lead was
Suresh Iyer, and his escalation contact was Deepa Krishnan.

[11:02:30] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What's driving the migration now?

[11:02:45] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Three things. First, RBI mandated that all scheduled commercial
banks migrate to ISO 20022 messaging by March 31, 2025. Second,
our current infrastructure costs us ₹42 crore annually in
licensing and support. Third, our UPI transaction volume has
grown 340% year-over-year — we're processing about 18 million
UPI transactions per day now.

[11:03:50] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What's your current uptime SLA with Infosys?

[11:04:00] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
99.5% for the core banking application. But we've had three
major outages in the last 18 months. The worst was on
August 14, 2023 — Diwali eve — when the system was down
for 7 hours and 23 minutes. That outage affected 1.1 million
customers and triggered an RBI inquiry. The incident reference
was RBI/2023/CYB/0847.

[11:05:15] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Ouch. What caused that outage?

[11:05:25] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A failed batch reconciliation job that corrupted the transaction
ledger. We had to restore from a backup taken at 02:00 IST the
previous night. The root cause analysis was conducted by our
internal audit team led by Vandana Rao, and an external firm
called Grant Thornton India. Their engagement partner was
Aravind Subramanian.

[11:06:30] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Let's talk about data. How much data are we migrating?

[11:06:45] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Roughly 84 terabytes of structured data, plus 12 terabytes of
unstructured documents — scanned KYC forms, loan agreements,
cheque images. We retain transaction data for 8 years under
RBI guidelines and customer records for the life of the account
plus 5 years.

[11:07:40] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Who are the key stakeholders on your side?

[11:07:55] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The CIO is Meera Iyengar. Our CISO is Vikram Singh Rathore.
The Head of Retail Banking is Fatima Sheikh. And the Chairman
of the Board is Dr. Ramesh Subramanian — he's also on the
board of the National Payments Corporation of India, or NPCI.

[11:08:50] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What about regulatory obligations? Anything beyond the RBI
mandate?

[11:09:05] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Yes. We're subject to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act
of 2023, which came into force on August 11, 2023. We also
follow the ISO 27001:2022 standard for information security,
and we're audited annually by Deloitte Haskins & Sells. Our
last audit was on February 22, 2024 — it resulted in two
minor non-conformities.

[11:10:20] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Can you share the audit findings?

[11:10:32] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
I can share the summary. The first finding was related to
access control — specifically, 14 terminated employees still
had active VPN credentials. The second was about encryption
at rest — three backup servers in our Chennai data center
were using AES-128 instead of AES-256. Both were remediated
by April 5, 2024.

[11:11:45] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What about your network topology? Multi-region or single-region?

[11:11:58] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
We have primary data centers in Mumbai and Hyderabad, and a
disaster recovery site in Chennai. We're connected via MPLS
through Tata Communications. Our SWIFT infrastructure is
segregated — it runs on a separate network with hardware
security modules from Thales. The SWIFT BIC is BHRTINBB.

[11:13:00] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What's your team structure for the migration?

[11:13:15] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
We'll assign a core team of 22 people. The project sponsor is
Meera Iyengar. The program manager will be Sanjay Kulkarni.
We have two technical leads: one for the core banking side
(Ashok Reddy) and one for the integration side (Nandini
Bhattacharya). We'll also need a dedicated QA lead — that
role is currently vacant, and we're interviewing candidates.
One is a gentleman named Farhan Qureshi from HDFC Bank, and
the other is a lady named Lakshmi Venkatesan from ICICI.

[11:14:30] KENJI WATANABE:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What's your target timeline?

[11:14:42] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
If we sign by August 15, 2024, we'd want a phased rollout:
Phase 1 (pilot) in Q4 2024 at 12 branches in Maharashtra.
Phase 2 in Q1 2025 across 80 branches in western India.
Phase 3 in Q2 2025 for the remaining 155 branches. Full
cutover must complete by March 20, 2025 — ten days before
the RBI deadline.

[11:15:50] ANJALI DESHMUKH:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
One last question. What are your success criteria?

[11:16:02] ROHAN MEHTA:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Zero customer-facing outages during cutover. Migration
completed within 5% of budget — our approved budget is
₹280 crore. And CSAT maintained above 4.2 out of 5.
If we hit all three, this will be the smoothest migration
in our 78-year history.
