              DIRECT EXAMINATION

              BY MR. DELGADO:

      1       Q.  Good morning, Ms. Halloran. Could you
      2           please state your full name for the record?
      3       A.  Katherine Elizabeth Halloran.
      4       Q.  And your current address?
      5       A.  I live at 1428 Elm Street, Apt. 7B, Oakland,
      6           California 94612.
      7       Q.  How long have you lived there?
      8       A.  About three years. I moved in September 2021
      9           after my divorce from Thomas Halloran.
     10       Q.  Is Mr. Halloran also a defendant in this
     11           matter?
     12       A.  No, he's not. We've been divorced since 2022.
     13           He has nothing to do with Brightpath.
     14       Q.  Let's talk about your employment history.
     15           Where did you work before Brightpath?
     16       A.  I was at Meridian Data Systems from 2018 to
     17           March 2021. I reported to the VP of Engineering,
     18           a woman named Patricia Nakamura.
     19       Q.  And then you joined Brightpath?
     20       A.  Yes. I was hired as a Senior Data Analyst in
     21           April 2021. My hiring manager was Robert Chen,
     22           the Director of Analytics. He reported to the
     23           Chief Technology Officer, David Kumar.
     24       Q.  Who was your direct supervisor at Brightpath?
     25       A.  Initially it was Robert. Then in July 2022,

[Page 13]

      1           there was a reorganization. I was moved under
      2           a new manager named Sarah Goldstein. She was
      3           hired from a competitor — I think it was a
      4           company called Quantiva Analytics.
      5       Q.  Did you have any issues with Ms. Goldstein?
      6       A.  Yes. From the beginning. She made comments
      7           about my age, about my family situation.
      8           She once told me in front of my colleague,
      9           James O'Brien, that I was "not management
     10           material."
     11       Q.  Do you recall the date of that comment?
     12       A.  It was around October 2022. I remember because
     13           it was just before the company offsite at the
     14           Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn.
     15       Q.  Who else was present?
     16       A.  James O'Brien was there. And I think Sarah's
     17           assistant, a woman named Mei-Ling Chen, may
     18           have overheard it.

              MR. DELGADO:  Let me mark this as Exhibit 14.

              (Whereupon, Exhibit 14 was marked for
              identification.)

              BY MR. DELGADO:

     19       Q.  Ms. Halloran, I'm handing you what's been
     20           marked as Exhibit 14. Can you identify it?
     21       A.  Yes. This is a performance review that Sarah
     22           Goldstein wrote about me in January 2023. I
     23           received it on January 17th.

     24           MS. RAMANATHAN:  Objection. Relevance.

     25           MR. DELGADO:  I'll rephrase.

[Page 14]

      1       Q.  Did you contest this review?
      2       A.  I did. I filed a complaint with HR on
      3           February 2, 2023. I spoke with a woman named
      4           Angela Foster in Human Resources. She was
      5           based in the Austin office.
      6       Q.  What was the outcome of that complaint?
      7       A.  Nothing. I was told it had been "reviewed
      8           and closed." Then in April 2023, I was placed
      9           on a performance improvement plan.
     10       Q.  Who placed you on the PIP?
     11       A.  Sarah Goldstein. But she claimed it was
     12           approved by her manager, Michael Torres.
     13       Q.  Was Mr. Torres ever your manager?
     14       A.  No. He was the VP of Data Science. I never
     15           reported to him directly.
     16       Q.  Did you speak with anyone else about this?
     17       A.  I spoke with David Kumar, the CTO. He
     18           suggested I "give it six months." He also
     19           mentioned that Sarah was close friends with
     20           the CEO, Elizabeth Ashworth.
     21       Q.  Did you ever speak with Ms. Ashworth?
     22       A.  Once. In August 2023. I requested a meeting
     23           through her executive assistant, Tomoko
     24           Yamamoto. The meeting lasted fifteen minutes.
     25           The CEO told me she "didn't get involved in

[Page 15]

      1           personnel matters."
      2       Q.  What happened after that meeting?
      3       A.  In November 2023, I was terminated. The
      4           termination letter was signed by Sarah
      5           Goldstein and cc'd to Angela Foster and
      6           Michael Torres. The HR business partner
      7           who delivered it was a man named Daniel
      8           Osei.
      9       Q.  Did you receive a severance package?
     10       A.  Yes. Eight weeks of pay, conditioned on
     11           signing a non-disclosure agreement. My
     12           attorney, Rachel Zimmerman at Zimmerman
     13           & Patel, advised me not to sign it. I
     14           didn't.
     15       Q.  Who was your attorney at that time?
     16       A.  Rachel Zimmerman. She's with the firm
     17           Zimmerman & Patel in Berkeley.
     18       Q.  Let's discuss the events leading up to
     19           your termination. Did you ever access
     20           customer data without authorization?
     21       A.  No, I did not.
     22       Q.  Did you ever share Brightpath's proprietary
     23           algorithms with any third party?
     24       A.  No.

     25           MS. RAMANATHAN:  Objection. Asked and answered.

[Page 16]

      1       Q.  Did you ever communicate with reporters
      2           or journalists about Brightpath?
      3       A.  I spoke with a journalist at the Wall Street
      4           Journal. Her name was Eleanor Vance. That
      5           was in December 2023, after my termination.
      6       Q.  What did you discuss with Ms. Vance?
      7       A.  I discussed the culture of harassment at
      8           Brightpath. I also mentioned that David Kumar
      9           had raised concerns internally about a product
     10           called InsightPro that may have violated the
     11           California Consumer Privacy Act.
     12       Q.  Did you provide Ms. Vance with any documents?
     13       A.  I did. I provided her with copies of my
     14           performance reviews, my PIP, and my
     15           termination letter. I also provided an
     16           internal email from Sarah Goldstein dated
     17           August 3, 2023, in which she referred to
     18           older employees as "legacy overhead."
     19       Q.  Do you still have copies of those documents?
     20       A.  My attorney does. Rachel Zimmerman has them
     21           in a safety deposit box at Wells Fargo on
     22           Shattuck Avenue in Berkeley.
     23       Q.  Did you provide the same documents to the
     24           Equal Employment Opportunity Commission?
     25       A.  Yes. I filed a charge with the EEOC on

[Page 17]

      1           January 22, 2024. The investigator assigned
      2           to my case was a woman named Fatima Al-Rashid.
      3           She's based in the EEOC's Oakland office.
      4       Q.  What was the outcome of the EEOC charge?
      5       A.  I received a right-to-sue letter in
      6           June 2024. My current attorney, Benjamin
      7           Achterberg at the firm Achterberg & Lowe,
      8           filed this lawsuit in July 2024.

              MS. RAMANATHAN:  For the record, the

              MR. DELGADO:  Noted.

      9       Q.  Ms. Halloran, let's talk about your
     10           current employment. Where do you work now?
     11       A.  I'm at a company called Northstar Health
     12           in Emeryville. I was hired in September
     13           2024. My manager is Priya Deshpande.
     14       Q.  Are you asking for front pay in this case?
     15       A.  Yes. My salary at Northstar is $147,000.
     16           My salary at Brightpath was $185,000 when
     17           I was terminated.
     18       Q.  Did you seek other employment between
     19           November 2023 and September 2024?
     20       A.  I did. I applied to at least forty positions.
     21           I interviewed with six companies. One was
     22           Salesforce, one was Snowflake, one was
     23           Databricks, one was Stripe, one was a
     24           smaller company called Fivetran, and one
     25           was a startup called Anyscale. I was only

      1           offered one position — the one at Northstar.
      2       Q.  What was the salary range for those roles?
      3       A.  Between $160,000 and $200,000.

      5       A.  I did not receive any other offers.
      6       Q.  I have no further questions.
