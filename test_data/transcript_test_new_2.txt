                     --- MARGARET ROURKE, having been first duly
                     sworn, was examined and testified as follows: ---

                     DIRECT EXAMINATION

                     BY MR. WHITMORE:

        1       Q.  Please state your name for the record.
        2       A.  Margaret Anne Rourke.
        3       Q.  And your position at Rourke Industries?
        4       A.  I am the Chief Executive Officer. I've held
        5           that role since founding the company in 2015.
        6       Q.  Ms. Rourke, where is Rourke Industries
        7           headquartered?
        8       A.  We're based in Newark, New Jersey. Our
        9           manufacturing facility is in Elizabeth, New
       10           Jersey. We also have a distribution center
       11           in Allentown, Pennsylvania.
       12       Q.  Let's discuss the contract at issue. When did
       13           Rourke Industries first engage Solstice Biotech?
       14       A.  In August 2022. We were introduced through a
       15           broker — a firm called Meridian Partners. The
       16           broker who handled our account was a woman
       17           named Priya Raghunathan.
       18       Q.  Who at Solstice did you deal with?
       19       A.  Initially, their VP of Business Development,
       20           a man named Klaus Bergmann. After he left in
       21           March 2023, we dealt with their CEO, Dr. Elke
       22           Hoffmann. She's based in their Munich office.
       23       Q.  Did you meet Dr. Hoffmann in person?
       24       A.  Yes. Twice. Once in Munich in June 2023, and
       25           once at a trade show in Frankfurt in October

[Page 16]

        1           2023. My executive assistant at the time,
        2           a woman named Beatrix Novak, accompanied
        3           me to the Frankfurt meeting.
        4       Q.  Where is Ms. Novak now?
        5       A.  She left the company in January 2024. She
        6           relocated to Vienna for family reasons. I
        7           believe she's now working at a company
        8           called Red Bull.
        9       Q.  Let's talk about the dispute. What was the
       10           nature of the disagreement?
       11       A.  Solstice claimed we breached the exclusivity
       12           clause in Section 4.2 of the supply agreement.
       13           We disagreed. Our position was that Solstice
       14           failed to meet the delivery milestones under
       15           Schedule B. We engaged outside counsel —
       16           a firm called Kellerman & Voss — to review
       17           the contract. The lead attorney there was
       18           a man named Thomas Reinholt.
       19       Q.  And then you retained Mr. Whitmore's firm?
       20       A.  Yes. In September 2024, we retained Whitmore
       21           & Associates. Mr. Whitmore has been our lead
       22           counsel since then.
       23       Q.  Did you ever communicate directly with
       24           Solstice's board?
       25       A.  Once. I wrote a letter to their Chairman,

[Page 17]

        1           a man named Wilhelm von Habsburg. He never
        2           responded. I believe the letter was forwarded
        3           to their General Counsel, a woman named
        4           Charlotte Weaver.
        5       Q.  Ms. Rourke, let's turn to the damages claim.
        6           Can you explain the basis for the $47.3
        7           million figure?
        8       A.  Yes. That represents lost revenue from Q3
        9           and Q4 of 2024. Approximately $28.5 million
       10           is from the cancelled European distribution
       11           agreement. The remaining $18.8 million is from
       12           projected North American sales that never
       13           materialized.
       14       Q.  Did you consult with an expert on this?
       15       A.  Yes. Dr. Sanjay Venkataraman from the firm
       16           Analysis Group. He's based in their Boston
       17           office. His report was filed on November 8,
       18           2024.
       19       Q.  Did Dr. Venkataraman's analysis account for
       20           market conditions?
       21       A.  It did. He used a regression model based on
       22           industry data from Gartner and McKinsey. Both
       23           reports are cited in his expert disclosure.
       24       Q.  One final question, Ms. Rourke. Have you
       25           ever met Dr. Elke Hoffmann in person?

[Page 18]

        1       A.  I have. As I said, twice. Munich and
        2           Frankfurt.
        3       Q.  No further questions.

                     MS. LINDQVIST:  I have no questions
                     at this time.

                     THE ARBITRATOR:  We'll take a fifteen-
                     minute recess. Please return at 11:30.

                     --- CROSS-EXAMINATION ---

                     BY MS. LINDQVIST:

        1       Q.  Ms. Rourke, my name is Astrid Lindqvist. I
        2           represent Solstice Biotech. I'd like to ask
        3           you some questions about your testimony.
        4       A.  Of course.
        5       Q.  You mentioned that Rourke Industries engaged
        6           a firm called Kellerman & Voss in 2023. Who
        7           at that firm advised you?
        8       A.  Thomas Reinholt was the lead partner. He
        9           worked with a junior associate named Sofia
       10           Marchetti.
       11       Q.  Did Kellerman & Voss provide a written
       12           opinion?
       13       A.  They did. It was dated July 14, 2023. I can
       14           provide a copy.
       15       Q.  Please do. Now, Ms. Rourke, you mentioned
       16           Dr. Hoffmann. Have you read her deposition
       17           transcript in this matter?
       18       A.  I have not. My attorneys advised me not to.
       19       Q.  Did you communicate with Dr. Hoffmann after
       20           March 2023?
       21       A.  Only through email. My assistant — Beatrix
       22           Novak, who I mentioned earlier — handled the
       23           correspondence.
       24       Q.  Ms. Rourke, isn't it true that Beatrix Novak
       25           is now employed by Solstice Biotech?

[Page 19]

        1       A.  That's not true. She works at Red Bull, as I
        2           said.
        3       Q.  Let me rephrase. Ms. Novak is employed by a
        4           company called Helvetica Analytics, is that
        5           correct?
        6       A.  I have no knowledge of that.
        7       Q.  No further questions.

                     MR. WHITMORE:  Redirect, Your Honor.

                     THE ARBITRATOR:  Proceed.

                     --- REDIRECT EXAMINATION ---

                     BY MR. WHITMORE:

        1       Q.  Ms. Rourke, just to clarify — when did you
        2           last speak with Ms. Novak?
        3       A.  In January 2024, when she gave notice.
        4       Q.  Have you spoken with her since?
        5       A.  No. I've had no contact with her since
        6           she left.
        7       Q.  Thank you. Nothing further.
